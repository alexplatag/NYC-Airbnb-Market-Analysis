# NYC Airbnb Market Analysis

## Overview
An interactive dashboard analyzing over 48,000 Airbnb listings in New York City, 
exploring pricing patterns by location, borough, and room type, along with the 
most in-demand neighborhoods.

## Tools

## Key Findings

**1. Manhattan commands a significant price premium.**
Manhattan listings average $196.9/night, nearly 60% higher than Brooklyn 
($124.4), the second-most expensive borough. The Bronx is the most 
affordable at $87.5/night.

**2. Entire homes cost more than double a private room.**
Entire home/apartment listings average $211.8/night, compared to $89.8 for 
a private room and $70.1 for a shared room — a clear pricing tier based on 
privacy level.

**3. Demand is concentrated in a handful of neighborhoods.**
Bedford-Stuyvesant, Williamsburg, and Harlem lead in total reviews, suggesting 
these areas see the highest guest turnover and booking activity, making them 
strong candidates for targeted host acquisition or pricing optimization.
